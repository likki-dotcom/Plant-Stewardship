Student Plant Stewardship and Health Tracking System

Grow. Care. Record. Continue.

A student-driven system for continuous plant care, health tracking and responsible handover.

About the Project

Responsible stewardship for every plant

The system helps students take responsibility for plants, regularly record their condition, monitor care activities and transfer responsibility to another student while preserving the plant's history.
